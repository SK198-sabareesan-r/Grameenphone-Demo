- Internet packs can be activated through USSD, SMS, IVR, MyGP, WoW Box, customer service, the GP website and other authorised channels.
- Auto-renew is OFF by default for most packs. To turn it ON, send SMS "ON" to 25000 or dial *121*3042#. To turn it OFF, send SMS "OFF" to 25000 or dial *121*3043#.
- To check internet balance, dial *121*1*4#.
- To cancel an internet pack, dial *121*3041#.
- After the internet volume finishes and while validity remains, usage is charged at Tk 1.165 per MB (including VAT, SC and SD), up to Tk 5.825.
- Unused data is carried forward if the customer buys the same pack within the active validity period, or if auto-renew succeeds.
- Packs are not applicable for Skitto users.
