GP offers three ways to activate roaming:
1. Online. Have a debit or credit card ready with USD transactions switched on. Choose the roaming type (GP recommends Standard roaming, which includes voice, SMS and internet). Pay in USD to activate.
2. Agent support. Enter the mobile number on the activation page and an agent contacts the customer.
3. In person. Visit an authorised GP store with the offline activation form. Customers can download and fill the form before going.

Roaming types:
- Standard: voice, SMS and internet.
- Basic: voice and SMS.
- Data: data and SMS.
- SMS: SMS only.

Prepaid roaming details (from GP's prepaid roaming page, summarised from a search excerpt, not read in full):
- A filled international roaming subscription form is needed, and a copy of both sides of the customer's international credit card with the security code hidden. The cardholder signature must be on the back.
- The customer tops up a USD balance that is deducted from the shared card.
- Documents can be emailed to gproaming@grameenphone.com, or brought to a GP Centre.
- When Standard roaming is switched on, the internet limit is 5 MB by default. When the limit is reached, roaming data is turned off for a while.
- A customer can migrate between Standard and Basic roaming by dialing *121*6*6*2#.

Before travel, check the roaming coverage, tariff and packs for the destination country.
