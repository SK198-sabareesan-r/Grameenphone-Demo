Pack for travel to Malaysia (also valid in Thailand, Singapore and India):
- Name: 10 Days Travel Pack. Internet: 10 GB. Validity: 10 days from the time of purchase.
- Price: BDT 1599, shown as USD 13.33 on the roaming site, including VAT, SD and SC.
- Activation: dial *121*1599# or buy it in the My GP app under Menu > Roaming.
- Works for both prepaid and postpaid GP numbers on Standard roaming or Data roaming. Basic and SMS roaming customers cannot buy roaming packs.
- To check the remaining roaming internet, open My GP app > Services > Roaming, or dial *66*1*3#.

Other packs listed on the same page (check the site for the countries each covers):
- Daily Combo Roaming Pack: BDT 832.61. 10 minutes of outgoing and incoming calls, 5 SMS and 1 GB, for 1 day.
- 7 Days Internet Roaming Pack: BDT 2083.61. 5.25 GB including bonus, for 7 days.
- Island Roaming Pack (Maldives and Sri Lanka): BDT 2899. 3 GB for 7 days.

Rules for all roaming packs:
- Packs do not renew automatically. Buying the same or a new roaming pack clears the benefits of the previous one.
- The pack price is charged upfront.
- When the internet volume is used up, roaming internet is disconnected.
- Roaming calls use a 60-second pulse and pay-as-you-go charges apply after the bundle.
- Prepaid customers need at least 3 minutes in their bundle balance, or an equivalent USD amount in the roaming balance, to make or receive calls.
- Postpaid roaming bills can go above the limit because charging is offline.
- Availability depends on the roaming partner network in each country.
- To move from Basic or Data roaming to Standard roaming, dial *66*1*2# or use My GP.
- SMS roaming customers must switch SMS roaming off first, then activate Standard roaming.
