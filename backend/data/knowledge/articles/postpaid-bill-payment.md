- Postpaid bills can be paid in MyGP, or through bill payment channels. For some channels, add 78 before the mobile number (for example 78017XXXXXXXX).
- Eligible e-bill subscribers receive the e-bill within 24 hours after the bill cycle completes.
- Customers can check bill and usage history in MyGP: Account > Usage History (last 30 days).
