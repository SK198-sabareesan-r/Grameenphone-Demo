- 121: product and service customer service, Tk 0.50 per minute.
- 158: customer service for complaints, free.
- insta.service@grameenphone.com: queries, requests and complaints, free.
- Live chat: on the GP website's online customer service page, free. The page also lists the GP Facebook page and a SignLine service in sign language for speech and hearing impaired customers.
- 01711594594: hotline for calls from other operators' numbers, at that operator's tariff.
- 01700100121: customer service hotline for GP customers while in roaming, at roaming network tariffs.
- 20000: HealthLine, Tk 5 per minute.
- 24444: migration for Nishchinto, Bondhu and Djuice, free.

A 10-second pulse applies to these tariffs, and SD, VAT and surcharge apply.
