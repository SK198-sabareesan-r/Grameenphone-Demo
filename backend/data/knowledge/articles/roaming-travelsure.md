TravelSure helps a roaming customer avoid a large bill from unintended data use.
- IR status check (postpaid only): dial *121*6*6*1# from home or abroad. It is free, and a reply SMS shows the roaming bill status, international credit card status and activation status.
- Usage Alarm (postpaid only): dial *121*6*6*5*1# to switch on free SMS alerts about roaming spend. Dial *121*6*6*5*2# to cancel.
- TravelSure Internet: a fixed monthly roaming data limit of 5 MB (default), 20 MB, 50 MB, 200 MB, or unlimited. For the limited options the customer gets usage notifications, a temporary cut-off when the limit is reached, and the option to reconnect. The unlimited option has none of these.
- To set or change the limit, dial *121*6*6*3# and choose the limit. To check the pack and remaining volume, dial *121*6*6*3*6#.
- Every customer gets the 5 MB limit for 30 days by default. A limit is refreshed 30 days after it is set. The tariff does not change with the limit.
