Each GP value-added service (VAS) is stopped with its own SMS keyword or USSD code. These are examples from public GP pages. Always confirm the exact service name on the customer's bill first.

| Service | How to stop | Charge |
|---|---|---|
| Missed Call Alert | SMS STOP MCA to 26222, or dial *121*6*3*2# | BDT 10 per month. Free for postpaid. Subscribe and unsubscribe SMS are free. |
| News Service (breaking news) | SMS Stop b to 22321, or dial *22321*3*1*2# | BDT 2 per day, plus charges |
| News Service (Bangla breaking news) | SMS STOP bb to 22321, or dial *22321*3*2*2# | BDT 2 per day, plus charges |
| Study Line | SMS STOP followed by the keyword to 22255 (example: STOP DB), or dial *22255# | Keyword services cost BDT 1.39 to 2.78 each |
| Call Block | SMS Stop CB to 25678, or dial *121*6*4*4# | BDT 7 per week (prepaid), BDT 30 per month (postpaid) |

Charges shown are before surcharge, SD and VAT unless the page says otherwise.

Steps for an agent when a customer disputes an extra charge:
1. Find the service name on the bill.
2. Stop the service with its keyword or code, or guide the customer to do it.
3. A refund is a separate step. See the refund policy article.
