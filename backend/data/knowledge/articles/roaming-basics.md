Roaming types: Standard (voice, SMS and data), Basic (voice and SMS), Data (data and SMS), SMS (SMS only).

To activate a data pack while roaming:
1. Check the customer's roaming type. Standard and Data roaming customers can buy internet packs. Basic roaming customers can buy voice packs only. SMS roaming customers cannot buy roaming packs.
2. If the customer is on Basic or Data roaming, migrate to Standard roaming: dial *66*1*2# or use MyGP.
3. Customer opens My GP app > Menu > Roaming to activate roaming and buy a pack. Each roaming pack also has its own USSD activation code on the roaming pack page.
4. The pack price is taken from the account balance. Validity counts from the time of purchase.

Good to know:
- Roaming packs do not renew automatically. Buying a new roaming pack clears the benefits of the previous one.
- Calls need at least 3 minutes in the bundle balance or an equivalent amount in the roaming balance.
- Availability depends on partner networks in each country.
