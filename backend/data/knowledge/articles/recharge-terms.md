Rules from GP's recharge terms page:
- Any amount recharged beyond a pack price goes to the main balance.
- Airtime sales are final and not refundable.
- Whoever requests a recharge should wait up to 30 minutes for the SMS and email confirmation for the number.
- GP is not liable if the recipient number was typed wrongly, and no refund is given in that case.
- If the paid service is not delivered, the payment partner refunds the same amount.
- Online pack purchase: any prior subscription fee or emergency balance already used is deducted from the recharged amount first. The selected pack may then not activate.
- Limits: a recharge can be up to BDT 1000 for prepaid and BDT 50,000 for postpaid at one time. For pack purchase by bKash or card, the prepaid limit is BDT 20 to 1000 and the postpaid limit is BDT 20 to 50,000.
- Balance checks: dial *121*1*2# for minutes, *121*1*4# for internet, and *566# for the main balance.
- For questions about online recharge, payment or refunds, the customer can email insta.service@grameenphone.com or start a live chat on the GP website. For problems with a card payment, the page points to the payment partner's support (PortWallet or SSLCOMMERZ, depending on the channel).

Suggested steps when a customer says the recharge is not showing:
1. Check the main balance with *566# and look at the last recharges on the customer card.
2. Ask how and when the recharge was made and for the amount.
3. If it was an online payment, remember a subscription fee or emergency balance may have been deducted first, and the customer may need to wait up to 30 minutes for the confirmation.
4. If it is still missing, raise a ticket and share the email and live chat channels above.
